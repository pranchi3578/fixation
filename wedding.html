What to Wear

Formal — and a little more covered for the church.

In the church

Shoulders and knees covered for the Mass, please. A saree, a salwar kameez with a dupatta, an anarkali or a long dress all sit right; a light stole is an easy way to cover up for the service and take off afterwards. For men, full-length trousers with a collared shirt, or a kurta.

At the reception

The same, a little less formal if you like. A blazer is welcome in the evening but never required. Base 11 is indoors, so heels are perfectly safe.

Soft, muted tones · we ask you to leave white to the bride

Church Reception

Questions

What time should I arrive?

By twenty to four, please. The Mass begins at four o’clock and the doors are best left settled before the bride comes in.

How long does the Mass run?

Between an hour and an hour and a half. The reception begins at six at Base 11 in Pala, about half an hour away by road.

Is there parking at the church?

Yes, and there is more within a short walk in Ponkunnam town. Come a little early if you are driving in on the day.

Are children welcome?

Very. Please count them in the number when you reply so we can seat you together.

May I take photographs?

During the Mass, please leave the aisle to our photographers and take what you like from where you are seated. Afterwards, everything is fair game.

May I bring someone with me?

If your invitation is for more than one, yes. If you are not sure, put the number in your reply and we will make it work.

What about gifts?

Your presence is the whole of it. If you would still like to give something, please speak to either of us on the day.

My plans have changed. What now?

Send a second reply through the form above, or call us. There is no such thing as too late to tell us.

Ask Us

Anything at all — the road, a room, the time, what to wear.
