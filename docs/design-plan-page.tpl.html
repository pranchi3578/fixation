The idea in one line

Chungking Express is built out of misses: cans that expire before anyone eats them, two people who pass within 0.01 cm and don't turn around, a boarding pass drawn by hand for a flight that isn't booked.

A wedding invitation is the opposite document. So we don't decorate the page with Hong Kong — we take Wong Kar-wai's recurring devices and let the invitation resolve each one. That resolution is the concept. Everything below serves it.

02

Art direction

Not a Hong Kong pastiche. The setting is Kerala — monsoon green, tungsten bulbs strung on a wire, palms against a black sky. The photographs already contain exactly that.

What we borrow is not Wong Kar-wai's place, it is his lighting logic: one warm practical source against one cool ambient, and nothing else in the frame competing. Both photo sets already obey it — the sparklers and the string lights are the practicals, and everything else is ambient and gets pushed out of the way.

The invitation is an evening object. It is dark, it is quiet, and it opens once. This document is set in the same palette for that reason.

03

The grade

Three frames from the proposal, graded to one look. The script is committed as tools/grade.py and reruns from assets/source/, so the look is reproducible rather than hand-retouched.

1Expose down to 0.88

Wong Kar-wai frames sit dark. Everything after this is easier once the highlights have somewhere to go.

2Filmic S-curve at 0.52

Separation without clipping the reds — a straight contrast bump would have flattened the dress.

3Split-tone

Teal into the shadows (+38), tungsten into the highlights (+24 red, −36 blue). The two-source logic, applied as colour.

4Cool the ambient

A hue-selective desaturation of the 46° band — the timber wall, the amber spill — at 52%, leaving skin and the dress alone. This is the move that makes it read as the film: it strips saturation from everything except the one red thing in frame.

5Bloom

Bright-pass at 204, blurred 30px, screened back at 40%. This is what sells the sparklers and the bulbs.

6Grain and vignette

σ7 at 42%, then a 56% vignette to keep the corners in the dark.

The black-and-white frames get a tritone instead — night #080D0F → jade #22443E → tungsten #FFE9B9, pivoting at 88 so the jade stays in the shadows. The neon sign comes back amber rather than grey, which is the entire point of doing it.

The hero is step-printed for real. Three offset copies (7 / 15 / 26px), blurred proportionally and screened back at 50 / 28 / 15%, then masked away from an elliptical centre so the couple holds sharp while the room around them drags. Wong Kar-wai's signature done to the photograph rather than faked — the CSS version stays for the headline only.

Every photograph sits under a --night scrim at 55–70% wherever type runs over it. No text is ever placed on an ungraded, unscrimmed frame.

Device 04 — step-print

Applied to the photograph in tools/grade.py, and to the headline in CSS: three copies at .55 / .28 / .14, offset 7 / 15 / 24px, blurred 2 / 5 / 9px, resolving over 900ms on cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1).

A looping flicker is a novelty. A single stutter is a light warming up — and it only happens once, on the cover, where their own sign is being quoted.

Everything else ≤ 400ms. Under prefers-reduced-motion every animation collapses to a 120ms fade, and the page stays complete and readable with zero motion.

08

Mobile

Design width is 360px; verify at 320.

  • 100dvh, never 100vh — this repo has already paid for that lesson once, in ebf6ee4 Fix iOS Safari scroll stuck issue.
  • One gutter, declared once on the wrapper: padding-inline: 20px, with vertical space via padding-block — never a padding shorthand that quietly zeroes the sides.
  • Photographs get object-fit: cover with an explicit aspect-ratio, plus an art-directed crop at ≤ 480px so the couple stays centred when the frame goes tall. Ship 900px and 1500px in a srcset.
  • Tap targets ≥ 44×44px. Below 380px the two buttons stack full-width.
  • The peel canvas sizes to devicePixelRatio and uses Pointer Events only — the current mouse-plus-touch double binding is exactly where double-fire bugs live. touch-action: none goes on the canvas alone, not an ancestor.
  • No horizontal scroll anywhere. The boarding pass is the one element allowed its own overflow-x container.

Both duplicate tracks were byte-identical and neither was loaded by anything — every version from v1 to v4 already pointed at song_trimmed.mp3. Deleted: 29 MB → 9.7 MB. The stock PNGs were the other 4 MB, but index.html, index_v2.html and index_v3.html all still load them, so deleting them breaks three live pages. They go when those versions do — a separate call, not this one.

10

Accessibility

  • Audio off by default, one labelled toggle with aria-pressed.
  • The date is never hostage to an interaction: it is in the DOM from the start, and the peel auto-completes after 6 seconds, or is skipped entirely under reduced motion.
  • Every photograph gets real alt text describing the moment, not "image".
  • Every Malayalam run carries lang="ml"; the page is lang="en".
  • The current 🕡 and 📍 rows become real text labels, or are marked aria-hidden.

11

What we are not doing

  • Literal Hong Kong signage, cigarette smoke, a blonde wig, Cantonese text.
  • A film-grain video overlay, or a second script font.
  • A looping neon flicker.
  • A countdown that ticks seconds. A ticking clock is a deadline, and this piece's whole argument is that there is no expiry date.
  • Anything that makes the church address harder to read than it is on paper.
