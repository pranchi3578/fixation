# Brainback Learn — "Unmute"
### 60s hero · cut-downs 30s / 15s · paper world · how Brainback reimagines learning

## Look (revised)

**Doodles, not paper cut-outs.** Everything is drawn in ballpoint and marker
on a ruled notebook page, and redrawn every frame so the lines boil. And it
is literal: a laptop screen, a real video-call toolbar, a mic button with a
red slash, a cursor that hesitates, the Brainback tutor in its own tile,
and "Why?" in a speech bubble. The red tape and lantern below are replaced
by the slashed mic button and the tutor tile.

## The idea

In a class of sixty, a question costs courage. With a tutor that's happy to
be interrupted, it costs nothing. Ask enough questions for free, and you
become someone who asks.

**The film is silent until she speaks.** The class is wordless; the teacher
is gibberish (ZooZoo-style). The first real word in the film is hers.

## The device

- **The mute tape.** In the paper world, "muted" is a strip of red paper
  tape across a tile's microphone. Unmuting means peeling it off.
- **The swallowed question.** Every question she doesn't ask becomes a
  Doubtling that she folds small and hides in her pencil box.
- **The tutor.** A paper lantern with Brainback's Glow inside. No face. It
  brightens when she talks, and it *stops mid-sentence* the moment she does.
- **Pace.** The lesson is a paper flipbook. In class it flips like a passing
  train. With Brainback it turns only when she turns it.

## Script

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| **0–4s** | An online class, as a wall of 48 paper index-card tiles. Every one has red tape over its mic. A teacher tile talks in fast gibberish. |
| **4–10s** | The lesson flipbook tears past like a train. Push in on one tile: **Meera**. A Doubtling rises in her tile and presses against the edge. |
| **10–16s** | Her finger hovers over the red tape. She glances around the grid: 47 faces. She pulls her hand back. She folds the Doubtling tiny and slips it into her pencil box. |
| **16–20s** | Teacher: gibberish that ends on a rising note ("*…questions?*"). Silence. 48 tiles go dark, one by one. |
| **20–24s** | Night. Meera opens the pencil box: it's stuffed with squashed, folded Doubtlings. Weeks of them. One wriggles out. |
| **24–30s** | Brainback: one tile, just her and the lantern. Out of habit, her hand goes to the red tape again. The lantern waits, a slow patient pulse. She peels the tape off. **First word of the film, in her voice: "Why?"** |
| **30–42s** | **Interruption montage.** The lantern explains; she cuts in with "wait—". It stops instantly and turns to her, brighter. "But why?" Again. She flips the flipbook back a page; it waits. Each question lets one Doubtling out of the box, and it flies off as a plane. Her whisper becomes a voice; her shoulders come up. |
| **42–46s** | The pencil box is empty. She's grinning, and asking faster than it can answer. |
| **46–54s** | Next day, the grid again. "*…questions?*" Silence. Then one red strip peels off: hers. She asks. Beat. Across the grid, tape starts peeling on other tiles, one, then five, then twenty. Paper planes cross from tile to tile. |
| **54–60s** | End card: **"Ask anything. Interrupt anytime."** Brainback logo. Four-note sting. |

**Taglines to test:** "Brave starts with a question." · "Learning at her pace, not the class's." · "Never too shy to ask."

## Cut-downs

- **30s:** beat 3 (hand pulls back) → beat 6 (peels tape, "Why?") →
  interruption montage → end card.
- **15s:** beat 6 → two interruptions → end card.

## Three shorts

1. **Interrupt Me** — The lantern starts explaining. She interrupts. It
   stops, pleased. She interrupts again. It glows brighter each time.
   *"The only tutor that likes being interrupted."*
2. **The Speed Breaker** — The class flipbook roars past like a train. Her
   own flipbook moves like a bullock cart at her pace, and she's smiling.
3. **The Pencil Box** — One week of unasked questions, and one evening
   emptying them.

**Not named or shown:** Google Meet or any real video-call brand. The class
is a generic grid of paper tiles, with no logos or recognisable interface.

## What production reuses and builds

- **Reuses:** the Doubtling ("?" → "!" → plane), night lighting, the Glow,
  and the four-note sting.
- **Builds:** Meera (jointed cut-out), a 48-tile corkboard grid with paper
  classmates, the red mute tape, the teacher tile, the lantern tutor, the
  pencil box, and the flipbook.
- **Voice:** one line from Meera ("Why?", "wait—", "but why?"). It needs a
  real voice actor with consent, or a synthesised voice clearly credited as
  such. The tutor's voice is only murmur under the music.
