# Brainback Learn — the parents' film
### "The Doubts You Can't Answer" · 45s, cut-downs 20s / 15s · paper world, wordless

The buyer is the parent. The student film sells *relief from doubt*; this
one sells a parent *relief from guilt and worry*: the doubt they can't help
with, and the phone they don't trust.

## The insight

- Indian parents want to sit with their child's homework and increasingly
  can't: the syllabus changed, and "we studied it differently".
- They worry the phone is stealing their child's evenings.
- They can't see what's happening behind the bedroom door.

## Cast

- **Amma** — a paper cut-out mother, printed-paper sari, bun of black paper.
- **Arjun** — her teenage son, paper cut-out, school shirt.
- **The Doubtlings** — as in the student film.
- **The Glow** — Brainback's light on the phone.

## Script

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–5s | 11 PM. Amma does the last round of the paper house, switching off lights. A line of light under Arjun's door. |
| 5–10s | She peeks in. Arjun on his phone. She frowns: *the phone, again.* Doubtlings piled on his desk; one sits on his head. |
| 10–17s | She marches in to help. Picks up a Doubtling and unfolds it: a page of trigonometry. She turns it sideways. Upside down. Her paper bun slowly curls into a "?". *(the laugh)* |
| 17–27s | Arjun turns the phone to her: Brainback. A lesson plays; he taps a follow-up; the Glow changes colour. The Doubtling in *her* hands straightens to "!" and flies out of her palms. |
| 27–33s | She takes the phone, suspicious, and scrolls: lessons, a quiz, no comments. Her bun uncurls. She nods. |
| 33–40s | She tucks him in, switches off his light. In her room, her own phone glows; a paper plane lands on her pillow: **"Arjun cleared 3 doubts today."** She smiles, sleeps. |
| 40–45s | End card: **"For every doubt you can't answer."** Brainback logo. Four-note sting. |

## Cut-downs

- **20s:** beats 3 → 4 → end card. The joke and the answer.
- **15s:** beat 3 alone → end card. Ends on Amma's "?" bun.

## Three shorts for parents (12–15s)

1. **Dad's Maths** — Dad starts to explain confidently, draws on paper, gets
   lost; a Doubtling pats his shoulder. Brainback's Glow. End card.
2. **Behind the Door** — Parents' shadows at the door, worried. Inside, the
   feed is quizzes. Their shadows relax. *(safe, no comments)*
3. **The Report** — A paper plane from the child's room glides down the
   corridor to the parents' tea table every evening. *(progress for parents)*
