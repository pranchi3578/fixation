# Audio

Drop the track here and set `TRACK.src` in `script_ce.js` to match.

## The Chungking Express options

| Track | Artist | In the film |
|---|---|---|
| *California Dreamin'* | The Mamas & the Papas | Faye plays it at the Midnight Express, loud and on repeat |
| *夢中人* (Dreams) | Faye Wong | her Cantonese cover of The Cranberries — the falling-in-love music |
| *Things in Life* | Dennis Brown | Cop 223's half of the film |

For a wedding invitation *夢中人* is the better fit: it is the tender one, and
it is the song attached to the character falling in love rather than to the
noise she hides behind. *California Dreamin'* is the more recognisable.

## Licensing

This page will be served publicly from GitHub Pages, so the recording needs
to be one you are entitled to publish. Options, roughly in order of least
friction:

1. **Buy the track** (iTunes, Bandcamp, Qobuz) and accept that a personal
   invitation page is a small private use. This is what most wedding sites
   do. It is not a licence, and it is your call to make.
2. **Licence it properly** — a micro-sync licence through the publisher, or
   a service like Lickd that clears popular music for online use.
3. **Use a cover or an instrumental** from a royalty-free library. A piano
   cover of *Dreams* carries the reference without the master rights.
4. **Keep `song_trimmed.mp3`** — the Amen track that is already here, which
   is what the fallback plays.

## Format

Trim to 60–120 seconds and export mono MP3 at 128 kbps. The file loops, so
nobody hears the end; a 4 MB track is 4 MB every guest pays for. Name it
whatever you like and point `TRACK.src` at it.
